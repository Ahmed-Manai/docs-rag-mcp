# Your first week

Welcome! This checklist covers what happens during your first five days.

## Day one

Your manager meets you at 9:30 and gives you your laptop. IT sets up your accounts in the morning, so plan to spend the afternoon installing your tools.

## Accounts and access

You get access to email, chat, the code repositories and the expenses app on day one. Access to production systems needs a security training, which you can take from day two. It takes about an hour.

## Your buddy

Every new employee gets a buddy from another team. Your buddy is the person to ask the questions you don't want to ask your manager, and they take you to lunch on your first day.

## End of the first week

On Friday you meet your manager to agree on goals for your first 90 days.
