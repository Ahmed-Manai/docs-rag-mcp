# Remote work

Everyone can work from home up to three days a week. Teams agree together on one shared office day, so people who live far away can plan their week.

## Working from another country

You can work from another country for up to 30 days a year. Tell your manager and HR two weeks in advance, because some countries need paperwork for tax and insurance.

## Home office equipment

New employees get a 400 EUR budget for a chair, desk or screen. Laptops, headsets and keyboards are ordered through the IT portal and stay the property of the company.

## Core hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours, you choose when you work, as long as you join your team's meetings.
