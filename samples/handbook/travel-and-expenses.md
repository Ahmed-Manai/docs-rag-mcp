# Travel and expenses

This page explains what you can spend when you travel for work and how to get paid back.

## Booking travel

Book flights and trains through the travel portal at least 14 days before you leave. Economy class is the default. For flights longer than 6 hours you can book premium economy.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is 150 EUR in most cities and 220 EUR in London, Paris, New York and Tokyo. Breakfast is included in the limit. If the only hotel near the event costs more, ask your manager to approve it in the travel portal before you book.

## Meals

You can claim up to 60 EUR per day for meals while travelling. Alcohol is not covered.

## Getting paid back

Submit your receipts in the expenses app within 30 days of returning. Refunds are paid with your next salary, or within ten business days for amounts over 500 EUR.
